We've redesigned the whole app. The translation screen, voice input, history, and language picker have all been refreshed to look better and feel easier to use.

Table Mode has a new design. When you show a translation to the person across from you, the text is larger and clearer, and long sentences now scroll so nothing gets cut off.

Voice input works better. Long speech is no longer cut short, and when you keep talking, the new words flow naturally onto what you already said.

Landscape is now supported. Rotate your device and both the translation screen and Table Mode expand to fill the wider layout.

Plus fixes for repeated translations, the language swap button, labels that appeared untranslated on some screens, and more.
