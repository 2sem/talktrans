Landscape & the banner question

Move the banner

Today it sits between input and output — exactly where the Enter Table Mode action wants to be, and it splits the conversation in half.

Anchor it to the bottom instead (below Translate, above the home indicator). The two language bubbles stay together and the primary CTA keeps the seam.

Never in Table Mode

Table Mode is the moment you hand the phone across the table. An ad on the foreigner-facing output looks broken and untrustworthy.

Hide the banner entirely here — and use it as a gentle nudge toward the ad-free upgrade (the app already has a "watch ad" reward flow).

Landscape

In landscape the two languages sit side by side; banner anchors along the bottom edge, full width.

Landscape Table Mode shows output only — one big line, upright, no input, no ad. Prop it up and let them read.
